### 网络策略纠正：不要求全面禁网

## Mailbox failure accounting and E2B network-policy review

The first mailbox version durably claimed valid parsed calls, but an absent,
malformed or oversized guest outbox file could stop the in-memory session
without leaving a per-call failure receipt. That is a logging gap, not a
prediction problem. The mailbox now writes an attempt record before reading
the guest file, records the raw-byte and parsed-request hashes when valid,
archives the failure type on any read/parse/broker/delivery error, rejects
replay, and checks its source hash on every call. The attempt, claim,
response, delivery and failure records are separate write-once files. An
oversized or missing request never reaches the broker. 31 relevant offline
tests passed; there was no E2B or GLM invocation.

## Broker-only A→B→A handoff, synthetic and offline

under an active supervisor cycle, it reads the first bounded raw decision
